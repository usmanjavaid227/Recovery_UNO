# 📖 HOW TO PLAY (For your audience)

## **QUICK START (30 seconds)**

1. **Select players** (2-4)
2. **Learn the rules** (instructions page)
3. **Play the game** (7-10 minutes per game)
4. **See results** (who won, how they won)

## **GAME FLOW**

```
START: You get 7 trash cards in hand

EACH TURN:
1. Look at current bin (plastic, cigarette, food, microplastic)
2. Match your trash card to the bin
3. Play → Draw random wildlife card (1-7 points)
4. OR Change bin (costs +1 trash card)
5. OR Sacrifice endangered animal (clears 3 trash, +10-5 = +5 net)

WIN CONDITION:
→ First to discard all trash cards
→ BONUS: Save all 4 endangered animals (+50 points)
```

## **STRATEGY CHOICES**

**Fast Aggressive:**
- Use wild cards early
- Finish trash quickly (+20 bonus)
- Species extinct (-50 conservation bonus)

**Slow Conservative:**
- Save wild cards
- Finish trash slowly (no speed bonus)
- All species thrive (+50 conservation bonus)

**Balanced:**
- Use some, save some
- Try to get both bonuses
- Maximum score!

- Patience pays off
- Extraction is tempting but unsustainable
- Protection is harder but rewarding
