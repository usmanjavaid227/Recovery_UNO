# 🌍 RECOVERY UNO - QUICK PLAY GUIDE

## GOAL
**Be first to discard all trash cards**
(BONUS: Save all 4 endangered animals for +50 points)

---

## SETUP
- 2-4 players
- Each player starts with **7 trash cards**
- 1 current bin to match

---

## EACH TURN

### STEP 1: Look at the Current Bin
- Plastic 🔵 / Cigarette 🚬 / Food 🍔 / Microplastic 🔬

### STEP 2: Choose Action (Pick ONE)

#### **OPTION A: Play Trash Card** (Best if you have a match)
1. Select a trash card that matches the bin
2. Click "Play Trash Card"
3. Card is discarded
4. **You draw 1 wildlife card** (get 1-10 points!)

#### **OPTION B: Sacrifice Endangered Animal** (If you have one)
1. Select ANY trash card (no match needed!)
2. Click "SACRIFICE! Use Endangered"
3. **3 trash cards disappear** (cascade effect!)
4. **+10 points** BUT **-5 cost** = **+5 net**
5. Endangered animal is **gone forever** (extinct)

#### **OPTION C: Change Bin** (If you can't match)
1. Click "Change Bin"
2. Bin rotates to next type
3. **Cost: Draw 1 extra trash card** 📦
4. Your turn ends

### STEP 3: Next Player's Turn
Continue around the table

---

## WILDLIFE CARDS (Your Rewards)

### Regular Wildlife (1-7 points)
🐜 Ant = 1 pt
🦋 Butterfly = 2 pts
🐟 Fish = 3 pts
🦆 Duck = 4 pts
🐢 Turtle = 5 pts
🦅 Eagle = 6 pts
🐋 Whale = 7 pts

### Endangered Animals (10 points, but can be sacrificed)
🐼 Panda = 10 pts (can use as wild card)
🐘 Elephant = 10 pts (can use as wild card)
🐯 Tiger = 10 pts (can use as wild card)
🦏 Rhino = 10 pts (can use as wild card)

---

## SCORING

### How You Win

**1ST PLACE: First to discard all trash**
✅ Get +20 points bonus

**CONSERVATION BONUS: Save all 4 endangered animals**
✅ Get +50 points bonus

**FINAL SCORE: Highest total points wins!**

---

## STRATEGY

### Aggressive Strategy ⚡
- Sacrifice wild cards early
- Finish trash quickly
- Get +20 first finisher bonus
- ❌ Miss +50 conservation bonus

**Score:** ~20-40 bonus points

### Conservative Strategy 🐼
- Save all wild cards (don't sacrifice)
- Finish trash slowly
- Get +50 conservation bonus
- ❌ Miss +20 first finisher bonus

**Score:** ~50-90 bonus points (BETTER!)

### Balanced Strategy ⚖️
- Use some wild cards, save some
- Try to finish fast AND save species
- Get both bonuses!

**Score:** ~70+ bonus points (BEST!)

---

## KEY REMINDERS

✅ **Trash cards = 0 points** (just cleanup)
✅ **Wildlife cards = your points** (1-10 each)
✅ **Match or change** (those are your only options)
✅ **Endangered animals = one-time power** (can use once, then gone)
✅ **Saving species = big payoff** (conservation bonus!)

---

## EXAMPLE TURN

**Your turn. Current bin: Plastic 🔵**

**Your hand:** 
- 🚬 Cigarette
- 🍔 Food  
- 🔵 Plastic ← This matches!
- 🔬 Microplastic
- 🐼 Panda (endangered)

**What you do:**
1. Select the Plastic card
2. Click "Play Trash Card"
3. Plastic is discarded
4. You draw wildlife → Get Turtle 🐢 (+5 pts!)
5. **Now you have 4 trash cards left**
6. Next player's turn

---

## END GAME

**When first player discards all trash:**

Game ends for everyone

**Final scoring:**
- Player who finished first: +20 points
- Player with all 4 endangered saved: +50 points
- Everyone: Keep their wildlife points

**Winner:** Highest total points!

---

## TIPS TO WIN

🏆 **Mix strategies** - Don't sacrifice everything OR save everything
🏆 **Time your sacrifices** - Use wild cards when you really need to
🏆 **Track endangered animals** - Know how many are left in the deck
🏆 **Plan ahead** - Can you save all 4? That's the big payoff!
🏆 **Balance** - Some speed, some conservation = best score
