Learn how to play & save endangered species

🔴 Trash Cards

Discard trash to clean the environment.

Points: 0 (no rewards)

Select → Click "Play" → Gone!

🟢 Wildlife Cards

When you play trash, you earn random wildlife.

Points: 1-7 (varies by species)

Same trash = different rewards!

🐼 Endangered Animals

Rare animals you can sacrifice for power.

Points: +10, -5 cost, clears 3 trash

Use once → disappears forever

📦 Change Bin

Can't match? Rotate to next bin type.

Cost: Draw 1 extra trash card

Strategy: adapt or pay the price

🏆 Win Conditions

⚡ First to Finish: Discard all trash first → +20 Points!

🐼 Conservation Bonus: Keep all 4 endangered animals → +50 Points!

💡 Speed wins +20. Conservation wins +50. Which strategy will YOU choose?

Good luck saving the planet! 🌍💚
